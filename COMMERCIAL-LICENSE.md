A Commercial Licence is the [PiranhaCommerce Attribution Licence](LICENSE) on the same terms, with one difference: **on the domains you have licensed, you
do not have to show the "Built on PiranhaCommerce" notice.** It is made by an order that names you ("you") and the domains ("Licensed Domains"). The
numbered sections below are the licence; this paragraph is only a summary.

1. **What a Licensed Domain is.** A Licensed Domain is the registrable domain named in your order (for example `example.co.uk`) **and every subdomain of it**
   (`shop.example.co.uk`, `admin.example.co.uk`, `eu.shop.example.co.uk` and so on). A different domain, even one for the same brand or the same
   shop in another country (`example.com` next to `example.co.uk`), is a separate domain and needs its own licence.

2. **What it allows.** On every Licensed Domain you may leave out the notice that section 2 of the Attribution Licence asks for, and may switch it off
   in the software's settings. Everything else in the Attribution Licence applies to you unchanged, including the permission to use and change the
   software however you like and the sections on warranty, liability and third-party components. On any domain that is not a Licensed Domain, the
   notice must still be shown.

3. **Test and staging copies.** A copy of a licensed site used only for development, testing, staging or demonstration, wherever it is hosted, is covered
   by the same licence as long as it does not sell to the public as a separate shop.

4. **Price.** The price is a one-off payment for each Licensed Domain, in pounds sterling, at the prices published at piranhacommerce.com when you order.
   The price of each domain depends on how many domains you hold licences for, counting the ones you are ordering and the ones you already hold:
   domains 1 to 4 at the standard price, domains 5 to 9 at 10% off it, and the 10th domain and any after it at 20% off. Prices do not include VAT, which is
   added where it applies. You pay within 30 days of the invoice, and the licence for a domain starts when it has been paid for.

5. **How long it lasts.** A Commercial Licence for a domain has no end date. It covers every version of the software, including versions released
   after you buy it, for as long as you keep to this licence. It ends for a domain if you do not pay for it, or if you break this licence and do not put it
   right within 30 days of the Licensor telling you.

6. **Agencies and clients.** You may buy licences for domains that you build or run for your clients. The licence belongs to the person named in the
   order, and you may transfer it to the client (or to whoever comes to run the domain) by telling the Licensor in writing. A licence cannot be used
   for a domain that belongs to someone else without that person's agreement.

7. **Changing domain.** If you stop using a Licensed Domain you may ask the Licensor, in writing, to move its licence to another domain you will use instead.
   The Licensor will agree unless there is good reason to refuse.

8. **Records.** The Licensor keeps a record of the Licensed Domains and who holds them, and does not publish it without your agreement.

9. **Refunds.** The price is not refundable once the licence has started, except where the law gives you a right to a refund.

10. **The rest.** Sections 3, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10 of the Attribution Licence (keeping notices, third-party components, contributions, no warranty, limit of
    liability, and the law of England and Wales) apply to this licence as well. If this licence and the Attribution Licence disagree about showing the
    notice, this licence wins for the Licensed Domains.
